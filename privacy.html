Privacy Policy — NutriVision AI

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Data we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with email or Google, we store your email address and name as provided by the sign-in provider, only to create your account and keep you signed in.
  • Tracking data: your meals, water, weight log and assistant chats stay on your device only. If you sign in, only your profile (name, age, height, weight, activity, goal, language, theme) syncs to your account in Supabase.
  • Photos: when you scan a meal or ask the assistant, the photo or message is sent to Google's Gemini service for analysis, with your consent, which you can withdraw any time in Settings. We do not store photos on our servers, but Google may process them under its free Gemini API terms.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • We never use your data for advertising or ad tracking.
  • We only request basic Google scopes: email, profile, openid.

Service providers

  • Sign-in and account storage: Supabase and Google.
  • Photo analysis and assistant: Google Gemini API.
  • Hosting: Vercel.

Deleting your data

You can export or erase your on-device data any time from the app settings (this covers device data only). To delete your account and synced profile permanently, email wwwhamiddd136@gmail.com.
